Privacy Policy for Obrix

Last updated: September 2026

Core Principle: Obrix strictly operates as a client-side analytics tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Obrix ("the Extension") only processes public product information available on Amazon web pages (e.g., ASIN, title, price, rating, review count, BSR ranking, variation specifications) that you actively visit. All parsing and analysis are executed 100% locally in your browser.

2. Personal Information

We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, browsing history outside supported Amazon domains, Amazon account credentials, payment information, or purchase history.

3. Permissions Justification

  • Host Permissions (*.amazon.com, *.amazon.co.uk, etc.): Used solely to inject the extension's script onto Amazon product and search result pages to read and display product data on the page.
  • Storage / unlimitedStorage: Used strictly to save your collected product data and user settings locally on your machine via Chrome's local storage API. Data never leaves your device.
  • SidePanel: Used solely to display the built-in Data Center interface inside your browser side panel.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data or extracted product data to any third party or remote server.
